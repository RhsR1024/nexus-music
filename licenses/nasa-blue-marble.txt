NASA Earth Blue Marble Imagery Notice
=====================================

The embedded equirectangular Earth texture (`src/providers/earthTexture.ts`) is
derived from NASA Visible Earth / Blue Marble imagery, distributed via the
three-globe example dataset:
- Source: https://github.com/vasturiano/three-globe/blob/master/example/img/earth-blue-marble.jpg
- NASA Media Usage Guidelines: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/

NASA imagery is generally in the public domain and not subject to copyright in
the United States. The image is resized to 1024x512 JPEG to keep app binary size
and decoded GPU texture memory bounded on mobile devices.
